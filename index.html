Harry's Zone 3 Round

Zone 3, Strawberry Hill. The streets Max has already done are ticked off below. The route covers every other street inside the border, with as little doubling back as the streets allow.

Tap a grey road or path to add it to the round, as far as the border reaches (up to the line). Remove it again from the street list. Tap the map where Harry starts. The route is worked out from there. Drag a corner to move it. Tap a small dot on an edge to add a corner. Double-tap a corner to remove it. The route redraws when you let go. Use + and − or pinch to zoom (Ctrl + mouse wheel on a computer); drag the map to move around when zoomed.

Streets inside the border

Tick a street when it is done and it drops out of the route. Where the odd and even sides are known you can mark just one side done.

Before you set off

  • Which side of the road. Each step says. "Both sides" means one pass, zig-zagging across. "Odd numbers" or "Even numbers" means do that side only; the route comes back along that road later for the other side, and the step says which step that is. Where the map has no house numbers it says "Left-hand side" or "Right-hand side" instead. This keeps crossings down on Strawberry Vale and Waldegrave Road.
  • Mallard Place is a private estate with several short roads. The route goes in and out of each one.
  • Michelham Gardens is a loop: in at one end, out at the other.
  • The route starts where the start pin is set and picks its own finish to keep the walk shortest. With no pin it chooses both ends itself.
